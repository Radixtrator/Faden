# What's new in Faden

## 0.14.2
- German reads more naturally: everyday words like Feed, Flair, Backup, Link and Sub, with a few r/ich_iel favorites kept (Lases, Pfosten, Faden, Hochwählen, Edith)

## 0.14.1
- Post options has a Save row, so you can save or unsave a post from the ⋮ menu
- List, Compact and Smaller cards can show Save, Hide, Share and Mod tools buttons under each post. Turn them on in Customize view › Buttons

## 0.14.0
- Share images and videos as files: the viewer's Share button asks whether to send the file or its link, and Post options › Share › ⋮ has an Image file or Video file row
- Load more comments works again: Reddit blocked the way Faden used to load them, so hidden comments are now loaded one by one
- Mark all as read no longer shows an error after marking your inbox read
- On a beta, the update notice offers the final release once it's out

## 0.13.0
- Autoplaying videos show how much time is left, and a button turns on their sound. Your music pauses while a clip plays with sound and resumes afterwards
- Zoomed pictures and videos glide on after you pan them, and stop at the picture's edges
- Always show download button: the image viewer can keep a download button on screen. Turn it on in Settings › Media
- The settings search finds settings by everyday English and German words, like "font", "mute" or "dunkel"
- The settings search shows your last searches when it opens
- Switch rows in Settings no longer get a tinted background when switched on

## 0.12.0
- Share to Faden: links, text and pictures shared from other apps open Create post, filled in
- Launcher shortcuts: hold Faden's icon for your first favorite community, Search, Inbox and New post
- Gallery posts: Create post takes up to 20 photos
- Polls show their options, and the results once Reddit shares them. Voting opens reddit.com, since Reddit's API can't vote
- Crossposts show the original post's media and text, with a row that opens the original
- Streamable clips play in Faden's own player, with sound. YouTube previews show the video's title and open in the YouTube app
- Remind me: Post options can bring a post back as a notification later
- Labels on saved posts: give saved posts and comments your own labels, and filter your Saved tab by them
- Autoplay can differ per view, for example only in Swipe mode: Customize view has an Autoplay choice for Cards, Slides and Swipe mode
- Volume key navigation: the volume buttons can jump between top-level comments. Turn it on in Settings › Comment options
- Live comments refresh rate has Sync's nine choices, from every 2 to every 60 seconds
- Flair filters also hide posts by the author's flair, not only the post's

## 0.11.0
- Autoplay: videos and GIFs can play muted in Cards and Slides as you scroll, one at a time. Turn it on in Settings › Data options › Autoplay
- Videos can close with a single tap, like images: Settings › Media › Close videos by
- A comment you've just posted or edited shows its text instead of raw HTML

## 0.10.0
- Searching shows your recently visited communities and recent searches: tap Explore's search field, or open Search before typing
- Explore and the navigation drawer have a Feeds section with Frontpage, Popular and All. Hide it from Explore's ⋮ menu if you don't want it
- Comment threads end at the last reply instead of running on to the bottom of the card

## 0.9.0
- Tap a post's flair to see the community's posts with that flair
- Below the last comment, the page shows the background color instead of looking like one long card
- Support Faden: a link to donate on Ko-fi, under Settings › Everything else. Faden stays free either way
- Pictures linked in comments from sites other than Reddit, imgur and Giphy wait for a tap before loading, so those sites can't see who's reading
- Moving to a new phone no longer copies your Reddit logins; log in again there. Settings and history still come along
- Undo last restore stays available for 7 days, and goes away when you log out

## 0.8.1
- Comment threads are rounded at both ends, so a collapsed comment is a neat rounded panel instead of square at the bottom

## 0.8.0
- Faden has its own look: blue with orange accents, the Figtree typeface, rounder cards and a new app icon. Prefer the old colors? Pick Classic in Settings › Theme management
- Communities and profiles show their banner at the top of the page
- Comment threads are drawn as threads, each reply looping off its parent, and top-level threads sit on their own panels
- Scores and comment counts sit in small pills, and post titles are bolder
- New icons throughout, a slimmer navigation bar and rail, and new loading animations
- Accent colors are generated so text stays readable in light, dark and black; downvotes are now violet
- Settings › View type opens Customize view directly, with the default layout and media preview size in it
- Faster scrolling and theme changes, lighter memory use in the image viewer, and videos that replay from a cache
- Better TalkBack support: toggles, choices, color swatches, search fields and the video seek bar announce themselves
- Drag the top sheet up to close it

## 0.7.0
- Resync is now called Faden. It's the same app, so your settings, accounts and updates carry on
- Pick the app's language in Settings › General: English or German (Deutsch, in r/ich_iel's words: Pfosten, Faden, Hochwähli)
- If the app crashes, it shows a report you can copy or share instead of closing

## 0.6.0
- Pictures posted in comments show in the comment instead of as a preview.redd.it link
- Faden tells you when a new version is out, and what changed once you've updated

## 0.5.0
- Reddit links from other apps open in Faden (allow them in Settings › Link handling)
- Search your settings from the top of the Settings page
- Comments posted since you last opened a thread are labeled "New"
- Changing a feed's sort only asks once whether to keep it
- Hold a comment to collapse it and tap it for options (Settings › Comment options swaps them)
